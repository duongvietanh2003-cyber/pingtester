Find your flow.

A simple practice. A measurable difference.

01

Your pace, your session

Choose solo practice for an uninterrupted flow, or switch on an AI race. Pick a word count (10, 25, 50, or 100) or a timer (15, 30, 60, or 120 seconds). Custom sessions support 1–500 words or 5–600 seconds. The clock starts on your first character.

02

Follow the caret

The mint caret follows every character. Press Space to move to the next word, and Backspace to correct a typo or revisit a previous word. Errors stay visible but do not block the next word. Word tests finish at the end of the final word; timed tests finish at zero, even mid-word.

03

Turn practice into progress

Completed tests earn 4 XP per correct whole word, plus 1 XP per five matched characters. Earn 10 bonus XP at 98% accuracy, and 2 XP per 15-key best combo (up to 20). Level 2 takes 100 XP; each next level needs 50 more. Restarts earn no XP. Your level is saved in this browser.

04

Precision has a pace

One word of speed equals five characters. Net WPM uses correctly matched characters; gross WPM counts every character attempt, including corrections. Accuracy counts correct attempts divided by all attempts. Space counts as a character; Backspace does not. AI races compare your final net WPM with your opponent's fixed pace.

Keep your rhythm: 15 consecutive correct characters light up your combo. A typo breaks it; backspacing reduces it. Paste and drop are disabled. The timer keeps running when you leave the tab. Esc resets; Tab then Enter restarts from the typing area. History, XP, and preferences stay on this device.
